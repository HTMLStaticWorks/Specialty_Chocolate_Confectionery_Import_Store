Core Foundation

SMART Goal Architecture & Progress Tracking

Vague goals like "Leo will improve reading skills by 80%" are impossible to enforce. We reconstruct your child's IEP goals using rigorous SMART criteria (Specific, Measurable, Attainable, Relevant, Time-bound) tied to clear data collection methods.

Deliverables Include:
  • Re-drafting 4–8 customized academic & behavioral SMART goals
  • Defining baseline testing protocols & quantifiable milestones
  • Setup inside your Parent Workspace Chart.js interactive tracker
  • Quarterly data audit to flag school non-compliance early

Evaluation Defense

Independent Educational Evaluation (IEE) Guidance

If a school psychologist's evaluation downplayed your child's autism, dyslexia, or ADHD, you have statutory rights under IDEA 34 CFR § 300.502 to demand an Independent Educational Evaluation at public expense. We guide you through the exact legal steps.

Deliverables Include:
  • Evaluation Critique Report pinpointing flaws in school testing
  • Formal Written IEE Request Letter tailored to your district
  • Independent Neuropsychologist & SLP provider vetting
  • Integration of outside evaluation results into the subsequent IEP
Package Rate:
$550
