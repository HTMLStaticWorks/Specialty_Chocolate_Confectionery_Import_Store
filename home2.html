Provenance Assurance

Zero-Meltdown Direct Cold-Chain

Specialty chocolate is a living, temperamental confection. Exposure to temperatures above 20°C ruins cocoa crystal structures and dulls delicate flavor volatiles.

  • Hermetically Sealed Vacuum Shippers: Insulated with botanical foam and phase-change cool packs.
  • Direct Cargo Air Freight: 48-hour transit from European chocolateries to our Madison Avenue hub.
  • 100% Quality & Temper Guarantee: If a single praline arrives compromised, we replace the entire box.
