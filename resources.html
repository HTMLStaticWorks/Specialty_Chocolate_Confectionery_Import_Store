Special Ed Lexicon

Special Education Acronym Glossary

School teams love using acronyms that confuse parents. Here is your quick cheat sheet to speak their language with confidence.

IDEA
Individuals with Disabilities Education Act

The primary federal special education law guaranteeing children with disabilities the right to FAPE.

FAPE
Free Appropriate Public Education

The core legal requirement that special education and related services must be provided at no cost and tailored appropriately.

LRE
Least Restrictive Environment

The legal mandate that students with disabilities must be educated with peers without disabilities to the greatest extent possible.

PWN
Prior Written Notice

Formal document school districts MUST give parents whenever they propose or refuse any identification, evaluation, or placement action.

IEE
Independent Educational Evaluation

An evaluation conducted by a qualified independent examiner not employed by the school district, often paid for by the district.

BIP / FBA
Behavior Intervention Plan & FBA

Functional Behavior Assessment (FBA) identifies the root cause of behaviors, followed by a BIP outlining positive supports.
