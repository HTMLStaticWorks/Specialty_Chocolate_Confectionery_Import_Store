Our Mission & Heritage

Empowering Parents. Demystifying the Law. Championing Every Child.

Sage Educational Advocacy was founded on a simple truth: parents shouldn't have to become legal scholars or fight an exhausting bureaucratic system just to get their child the education they deserve.

  • Child-First, Always: Every negotiation, accommodation request, and goal edit revolves solely around the student's holistic well-being and growth.

Legal Pillars

5 Non-Negotiable Parent Rights Under IDEA

Federal law grants parents extensive authority in special education decisions. Here are five statutory pillars we vigorously defend.

01
FAPE (Free & Appropriate Public Education)

The school must provide individualized instruction tailored to confer meaningful educational benefit — not merely "the bare minimum" (Supreme Court ruling in Endrew F. v. Douglas County).

02
LRE (Least Restrictive Environment)

Children must be educated alongside non-disabled peers to the maximum extent appropriate, with supplementary aids and services before any self-contained placement is considered.

03
PWN (Prior Written Notice)

If the school proposes or refuses to initiate an evaluation, accommodation, or service change, they MUST provide written explanation citing specific evaluation data.

04
Independent Educational Evaluations (IEE)

If you disagree with a district evaluation, you have the right to request an outside independent evaluation at public expense under 34 CFR § 300.502.

05
Equal Partnership in IEP Meetings

Parents are legally equal team members. The school cannot present "predetermined" IEPs or finalize placements without genuine parent participation and input.
