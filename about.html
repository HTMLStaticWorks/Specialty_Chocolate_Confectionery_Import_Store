The Founding Vision

Rescuing the Crystal Temper of Fine Chocolate

Most imported chocolate suffers in standard cargo holds: micro-fluctuations in moisture and temperature break the delicate Beta-V crystal matrix of pure cocoa butter, resulting in bloom, chalky textures, and evaporated aromatic volatiles.

In 2012, our founders created an unbroken, climate-monitored air-cargo cold chain. By flying direct micro-lots at precisely 16°C and 50% relative humidity from Paris, Zürich, and Brussels, we deliver confectionery in the exact pristine state the master chocolatiers intended at the moment of cooling.

Standard of Integrity

Our Four Pillars of Quality

Every confectionery item bearing the Maison du Cacao seal adheres to rigorous sensory and ethical benchmarks.

Single-Origin Terroirs

We never blend origins. Each tablet celebrates the volcanic soils of Chuao, the rainforests of Esmeraldas, or the high-acidity valleys of Sambirano.

16°C Cold-Chain Flight

Every parcel travels in active temperature-controlled containers via scheduled air cargo with continuous digital IoT temperature loggers.

Sommelier Sensory Audits

Our Certified Master Sommeliers blind-taste each arrival to verify acidity notes, melt rate, snap acoustics, and finish persistence.

Direct Farmer Prosperity

We pay 250% to 400% above Fair Trade minimums directly to cooperative farmers, investing in biodiversity, schools, and heirloom conservation.
