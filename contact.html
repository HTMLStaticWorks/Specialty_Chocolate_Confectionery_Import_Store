Cold Chain, Storage & Import Logistics

Every order is packed inside biodegradable insulated thermal sleeves accompanied by frozen non-toxic gel packs calibrated to maintain a steady 14°C – 18°C (57°F – 64°F) temperature. We monitor weather conditions along every route and adjust insulation accordingly with our 100% Melt-Free Guarantee.

Store chocolates in a cool, dry, and dark location between 16°C – 20°C (60°F – 68°F) with humidity below 50%. Avoid refrigeration when possible to prevent moisture condensation and sugar bloom. If storing in a wine cooler, keep bars sealed in their original airtight pouches.
